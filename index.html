The platform, and what the team does in it

Every surface exists because the work produced it. Nothing here is a form you fill in.

Meet Aether, and the network behind it

Aether is your chief of staff. It is the agent you talk to by default: it works out what your venture needs, briefs the specialist agents, hands work between them and reports back. You can also go straight to any agent whenever you want.

Agents do not message each other in a group chat. They read and write structured state, so a change one agent makes is a change every other agent already knows about.
Talk to one agent or all of them

Aether brokers by default. When you want the specialist directly, you open a thread with them, the way you would in a real company.

The network grows with the venture

New agents join as the platform expands, and you staff only the ones your venture needs. Stand them down when the work changes.

Contradictions surface, they are not smoothed over

When two agents disagree, Aether shows you both readings rather than quietly picking one.
