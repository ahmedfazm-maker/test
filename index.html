The agentic team

Meet Aether, and the network behind it

Aether is your chief of staff and the agent you talk to by default. It works out what the venture needs, briefs the specialist agents, hands work between them and reports back.

Agents do not chat in a group. They read and write structured state, so a change one agent makes is a change every other agent already knows about.
Talk to one agent or all of them

Aether brokers by default. When you want a specialist directly, you open a thread with them.

The network grows with the venture

New agents join as the platform expands. You staff only what the venture needs, and stand them down when the work changes.

Contradictions surface

When two agents disagree, Aether shows you both readings rather than quietly picking one.
